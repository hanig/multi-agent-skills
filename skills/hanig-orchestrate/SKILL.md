## Operate until quiescent

The operating cycle is:

1. Reconcile coordinator state and current remote observations.
2. Service stopped or permission-blocked workers and preserve endangered work.
3. Commission or read review, reproduce findings, and record dispositions.
4. Merge only an eligible head, record the observation, and advance once.
5. Drain pending tracker intents through the authorized connector.
6. Reconcile the tracker by state, dispatch work that is newly ready, and record
   every covered issue's move to in progress with its unit before reporting; if
   the connector is unavailable, retain and report the pending synchronization
   without blocking unrelated dispatch.
7. Publish the ordered three-part report.

`swarm.py advance` is one locked, idempotent pass and then exits. A deterministic
host scheduler supplies repeated unattended calls; it is not a resident loop.
The broader session-side cycle above includes the network and judgment work the
coordinator deliberately cannot perform. See [operating loop](references/operating-loop.md).

Yield only at completion with the required report, a precise owner decision or
permission request, an external wait with a registered wakeup, or an exhausted
execution budget with a durable handoff. Instructions cannot keep a terminated
session alive.

## Delegate complete ownership

A delegated code unit owns one issue through implementation, tests, mutation
check, review, reproduced findings, dispositions, preservation, push, and pull
request. Its prompt names the objective, acceptance criteria, source material,
constraints, declared outputs, exact test command, review claims, mutation,
recovery, and coordinator completion protocol. Provider, model, mode, thinking,
and environment remain plan fields rather than prompt prose.

A retry is a fresh attempt at that whole unit. A continuation begins from a
durable recovery ref, names what is already complete and reviewed, and asks only
for the remaining bounded work. Size units by acceptable repeated work, never by
presentation stages or by separating implementation from its review obligation.
The checked measurements and a prompt checklist are in [delegation and evidence](references/delegation-evidence.md).

## Review and adjudicate

Use the enabled flags and profile membership in the loaded review gate's
`reviewers.json`; prose rosters and remembered prices are not routing truth.
Keep the author off the panel. Report who actually answered, which reviewers
were unavailable, and `REVIEW_PASS` only as failure by that named panel to
refute the claims.

Every implementation round asserts the actual delivered claims plus the
counter-claim that the change cannot make an honest run fail. Reproduce each
finding before acting. Preserve the original verdict and finding even when the
disposition is disproved or nonblocking.

The disposition matrix is fixed:

- Disproved by contradictory evidence: dismiss with that evidence.
- Confirmed violation of a hard invariant or required criterion: repair.
- Confirmed and demonstrably outside required behavior: nonblocking only under
  the Astra concurrence rule and with the complete impact record.
- Unresolved, ambiguous, or outside delegated risk: block, investigate within
  budget, then ask the owner.

The gate has at most three substantive rounds per change. After the rounds are
spent, convene the step-back committee with the history and ask for an exit
criterion, not approval. Details are in [authority and adjudication](references/authority-adjudication.md).

## Evidence and reporting

Check checkable claims against their named sources. Worker files, issue text,
review prose, and handoff narrative are submissions or context, never authority.
Retain exact commands, outputs, heads, bases, findings, dispositions, panel
results, costs when actually measured, and stated limits.

Report on the declared cadence; while units are live the default mandate cadence
is hourly. The report has three ordered parts: running work with observation
times and separate liveness/progress/permission facts; tracker state swept by
state rather than recency; and work that can start now, followed by its dispatch.
If nothing can start, say why. Merging does not close an issue, so reconcile
again after every push, merge, and close.

## What remains outside this skill

This skill does not mechanically establish honest relevance judgments,
independent minds, external attestation truth, persistent liveness, tracker
delivery, or that a configured review price equals a completed round's cost.
Coordinator judgment and closure stay in `hanig-swarm`; review semantics stay in
`hanig-review-gate`; run reports stay in `hanig-project`; pointer capture stays
in `hanig-portable-handoff`.
